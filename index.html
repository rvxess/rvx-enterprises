About RVX Enterprises

RVX Enterprises is a New Delhi-based proprietorship established with a focus on online retail, e-commerce, trading and general supply.

Our working approach is simple: understand the customer's exact requirement, check suitable sourcing options, verify specification and availability, and then communicate a practical quotation before final commitment.

The business aims to support individuals, offices, commercial establishments and institutional customers for regular as well as requirement-based supplies.

PROFESSIONAL PROCESS

How RVX Enterprises Works

A structured sourcing process helps ensure that requirements, availability, specifications and commercial terms are understood before supply.

01

Receive Requirement

Product name, quantity, specification, preferred brand, location and required date.

02

Check Suitable Source

Suitable suppliers, dealers and sourcing options are checked according to the requirement.

03

Verify Availability

Product specification, expected availability, estimated delivery and relevant details are checked.

04

Share Quotation

A suitable quotation can be prepared after checking product cost, delivery and applicable commercial terms.

05

Confirm Order

Order processing begins after product, price, payment and delivery terms are mutually confirmed.

06

Supply & Follow-Up

Supply is completed with appropriate business records and customer follow-up as required.
