HOW WE WORK

Professional Supply Process

A simple requirement-based business process designed to confirm sourcing before final commitment.

1

Receive Requirement

Product, quantity, brand/specification, delivery location and required date.

2

Source & Verify

Check suitable supplier/dealer, availability, specification and expected delivery time.

3

Share Quotation

Prepare suitable quotation after checking product cost, delivery and applicable taxes.

4

Order Confirmation

Proceed after product, price, payment and delivery terms are mutually confirmed.

5

Procure & Supply

Arrange the confirmed product and complete supply with appropriate records.

6

Customer Follow-Up

Maintain invoice, payment and customer follow-up records for continued service.
