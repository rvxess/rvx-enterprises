Business Supply Model

A low-overhead, requirement-led approach designed to avoid unnecessary inventory and quote after checking product availability and supplier rates.

1

Receive Requirement

Product, quantity, specification, budget if applicable, delivery location and required date.

2

Source & Verify

Check reliable supplier/dealer availability, exact specification, invoice support and delivery time.

3

Share Quotation

Calculate product cost, delivery/freight, applicable taxes and a reasonable business margin.

4

Order Confirmation

Proceed only after requirement, price and delivery terms are agreed and sourcing is feasible.

5

Procure & Deliver

Arrange the confirmed product, check quality/specification and complete delivery with records.

6

Follow-Up

Maintain payment, invoice and customer follow-up records to support repeat business.
